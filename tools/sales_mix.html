Problem 1

Mabini Trading sells two lines, X and Y, which always move in a 2:3 ratio. X sells for ₱2,000 and costs ₱1,200 in variable cost per unit. Y sells for ₱1,000 and costs ₱700 per unit. Annual fixed costs are ₱585,000. How many units of each product must be sold to:

  1. Reach the break-even point
  2. Generate a pre-tax profit of ₱200,000
  3. Generate a net income of ₱350,000, given a 30% income tax rate
  4. Earn a pre-tax profit equal to 10% of sales
  5. Earn an after-tax profit equal to 12% of sales, given a 30% income tax rate
  1. 468 units of X; 702 units of Y
  2. 628 units of X; 942 units of Y
  3. 868 units of X; 1,302 units of Y
  4. 650 units of X; 975 units of Y
  5. 900 units of X; 1,350 units of Y

Problem 2

For every 3 units of Product M that Luzon Corp. sells, it sells 5 units of Product N. M has a unit price of ₱500 and a unit variable cost of ₱200. N has a unit price of ₱1,200 and a unit variable cost of ₱780. Fixed costs total ₱504,000 annually. Determine the unit sales of M and N required to:

  1. Neither earn a profit nor incur a loss
  2. Earn an operating income of ₱300,000
  3. Earn a profit after tax of ₱450,000 (tax rate: 25%)
  4. Earn an operating income of 5% of total sales
  5. Earn an after-tax income of 6% of total sales (tax rate: 25%)
  1. 504 units of M; 840 units of N
  2. 804 units of M; 1,340 units of N
  3. 1,104 units of M; 1,840 units of N
  4. 576 units of M; 960 units of N
  5. 630 units of M; 1,050 units of N

Problem 3

Visayas Manufacturing produces Alpha and Beta in a fixed sales mix of 1:4. Alpha sells at ₱800 per unit, with variable costs of ₱480. Beta sells at ₱1,800 per unit, with variable costs of ₱630. Yearly fixed costs amount to ₱1,955,000. What sales volume in units of Alpha and Beta is needed to:

  5. Realize after-tax income equal to 13% of sales revenue, assuming a 35% tax rate
  1. 391 units of Alpha; 1,564 units of Beta
  2. 431 units of Alpha; 1,724 units of Beta
  3. 591 units of Alpha; 2,364 units of Beta
  4. 425 units of Alpha; 1,700 units of Beta
  5. 575 units of Alpha; 2,300 units of Beta

Problem 4

  3. 4,077 units of P; 2,718 units of Q
  4. 4,575 units of P; 3,050 units of Q
  5. 4,275 units of P; 2,850 units of Q

Problem 5

Bonifacio Co. markets two products, K and L, and sells 5 units of K for every 2 units of L. K is priced at ₱700 with a variable cost of ₱560. L is priced at ₱1,000 with a variable cost of ₱600. The company's fixed costs are ₱985,500 per year. How many units of K and L must be sold to:

  1. Attain the break-even point
  2. Attain a pre-tax income of ₱300,000
  3. Attain a post-tax income of ₱450,000, at a 20% tax rate
  4. Attain a pre-tax income of 14% of revenue
  5. Attain a post-tax income of 12% of revenue, at a 20% tax rate
  1. 3,285 units of K; 1,314 units of L
  2. 4,285 units of K; 1,714 units of L
  3. 5,160 units of K; 2,064 units of L
  4. 6,750 units of K; 2,700 units of L
  5. 7,300 units of K; 2,920 units of L
